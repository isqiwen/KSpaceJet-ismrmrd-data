Citation Format
---------------

In manuscripts submitted for publication, acknowledge the use of OCMR by
citing the following article:

Chen et al., “OCMR (v1.0)—Open-Access Multi-Coil k-Space Dataset for
Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance Imaging,” arXiv:2008.03410 (2020).

In presentations or published abstracts, you may refer to the OCMR dataset as
“OCMR Dataset from www.ocmr.info”.
